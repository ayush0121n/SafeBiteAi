# Medical Safety Rules

- SafeBite AI does not diagnose medical conditions.
- SafeBite AI does not guarantee that a product is allergy-safe.
- “Safe” means no configured concern was found in the readable label text.
- “Avoid” means a user-selected allergen or strict rule was matched.
- “Uncertain” must be shown when OCR confidence is too low.
- Critical allergy decisions must display the matched label text.
- Cross-contact wording such as “may contain” must be shown separately.
- Users must be reminded to verify the original package.
- Emergency symptoms must direct users to local emergency care and their prescribed allergy plan.
