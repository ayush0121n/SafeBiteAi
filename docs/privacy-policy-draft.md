# Privacy Principles

- Collect only information required for label guidance.
- Make health-profile fields optional where possible.
- Do not sell user health or food-scan data.
- Let users delete profile, history, and uploaded images.
- Define image retention duration.
- Do not store uploaded images by default during local MVP mode.
- Explain whether images are processed locally or sent to the backend.
- Do not expose health data in frontend logs or analytics events.
