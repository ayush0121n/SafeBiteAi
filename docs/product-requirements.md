# SafeBite AI MVP Requirements

## Problem
Food labels are hard to read and understand, especially for people with allergies, dietary restrictions, diabetes-related goals, blood-pressure goals, parents, and older adults.

## Primary user goal
A user uploads a food-label image and receives an easy-to-understand summary of possible allergens and nutrition-related concerns based on their profile.

## Primary users
- People managing food allergies
- Parents helping children with allergies
- Adults managing sugar, sodium, or saturated-fat goals
- Seniors who need larger text and simpler explanations

## MVP capabilities
- Create and edit a health profile
- Select allergies and food preferences
- Upload JPG, PNG, or WEBP food-label photos
- Extract text from labels through OCR
- Detect matching allergens through rules
- Extract basic nutrition values when readable
- Show Safe, Caution, Avoid, or Uncertain
- Save scan history locally
- Enable large-text and high-contrast mode
- Read results aloud through browser speech tools

## Non-goals
- Diagnosing disease
- Predicting clinical outcomes
- Guaranteeing a product is safe
- Replacing package-label verification
- Replacing medical advice
